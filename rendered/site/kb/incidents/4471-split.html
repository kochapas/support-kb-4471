How the reports are counted

The way the desk counts, with its own code: groupByCause in causes.ts and the desk’s notes in desk-notes.json. The page adds no rule of its own for parser and hint.

  1. parser: the tenant writes the day first, and the report did not come through a booking-v3 form (causes.ts).
  2. hint: the tenant writes the day first, and the report came through a booking-v3 form, whose hint prints MM/DD/YYYY (causes.ts).
  3. shift: the desk read the report as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). A month and a day that trade places never land one day apart, so this is a problem of its own. The desk counts a report where it is filed and footnotes the ones it read differently, so these reports count under their cause and again here.
  4. unplaced: no tenant on file, or the tenant does not write the day first. No ticket until someone reads them.

Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The table shows it next to the problem so the gap stays visible: an en-US browser is not a booking-v3 form.

What each ticket asks for

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. For a tenant who writes the day first, any date with both numbers at 12 or under comes out with day and month swapped: booked 2 July, invite for 7 February. Fix: parse with the tenant’s locale, the way dayFirst() already asks the platform.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says and get day and month swapped. Fix: print the hint from the tenant’s locale. The parser fix does not reach this one.

shift: the date is right but lands one day early for some readers: the same meeting shows Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, and an invite can arrive “before” the booking. That is a date sent as midnight in one time zone and shown in another. Fix: send all-day dates as dates, not as instants.

For the desk

  • Matching on subject merged these (decision 0001). The same subject line turns up under different problems, so the subject cannot tell them apart. Read the body, or at least the tenant and the way in, before you merge.
  • Keep a report’s ticket with its problem. When a new report comes in for #4471, file it by cause as the desk always has, and add it to that problem’s ticket; a one-day move goes on shift too.
  • The numbers on this page are built from the reports at render time. When the desk merges more reports into #4471, they move on their own.
